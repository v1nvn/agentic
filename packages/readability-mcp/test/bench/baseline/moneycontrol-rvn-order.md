# Rail Vikas Nigam shares gain on order win worth Rs 903 crore- Moneycontrol.com

Rail Vikas Nigam Share Price | The market capitalisation of the comapny stands at Rs 44,202.43 crore.

September 07, 2026 / 09:30 IST

![](https://images.moneycontrol.com/static-mcnews/2024/04/Rail-Vikas-Nigam.jpg?impolicy=website&width=770&height=431)

Rail Vikas Nigam

[Rail Vikas Nigam](https://www.moneycontrol.com/india/stockpricequote/miscellaneous/railvikasnigam/RVN) share price was trading with marginal gains in the opening trade on September 7 following the company received a Letter of Award worth Rs 903 crore.

At 09:28am, Rail Vikas Nigam was quoting at Rs 213.50, up Rs 1.50, or 0.71 percent, on the BSE.

The company has received Letter of Award from SJVN Thermal (STPL), a wholly owned subsidiary of SJVN, for construction of a permanent siding along with an aerial connection via track from the Plant Boundary to Chausa and Pawani Kamarpur at Buxar (Phase-III) in Bihar for the 1320 MW Buxar Thermal Power Project (BTPP) of STPL.

The order value is Rs 903 crore and is to be completed in 36 months.
