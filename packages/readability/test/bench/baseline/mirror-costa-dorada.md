## A 45-year-old British man has drowned after jumping from a tourist ferry into the sea off Spain’s Costa Daurada, reportedly in an attempt to swim to a beach bar

**Rita Sobot** Spain Correspondent and [**Eliana Nunes**](https://www.mirror.co.uk/authors/eliana-nunes/) News Reporter

08:47, 06 Sep 2026Updated 12:48, 06 Sep 2026

A British holidaymaker has tragically drowned after jumping into the sea from a tourist ferry off Spain's Costa Daurada, reportedly in an attempt to swim to a beach bar.

Article continues below

The 45-year-old man was travelling with his brother and parents on the boat between the coastal towns of Cambrils and Salou in Catalonia, in the country's north-east, when the tragedy unfolded on Friday evening.

‌  

The man reportedly "threw himself from the window" of the ferry, which was just minutes from reaching the port of Salou, apparently intending to swim to the beach, according to local newspaper Diari de Tarragona. His 42-year-old brother is said to have followed him into the water but survived.

‌  

"The circumstances of the drowning are surprising, since the victim jumped into the water from a tourist ferry that makes the Cambrils-Salou route and vice versa (from port to port) voluntarily," states Diari de Tarragona.

The newspaper claimed that, shortly before jumping out of the window, the brothers had "wanted to drink beer on board" the ferry but were refused by the captain.

‌  

The crew alerted the emergency services at 6.41pm local time (5.41pm BST), reporting that the two men had jumped overboard. Maritime Rescue teams were then sent out to search for them.

The brothers' parents stayed on board the ferry and were reportedly planning to meet them at a beach bar. According to reports, they said their sons were strong swimmers and were confident they would be able to make it to the beach.

The man's body was recovered, with Mossos d'Esquadra officers cordoning off the area. A forensic doctor attended the scene at 9.25pm local time (8.25pm BST) to carry out the removal.

Article continues below

The Civil Guard in Tarragona is investigating the circumstances surrounding the death. The results of a post-mortem examination are awaited and are expected to establish the precise cause of death.

The man's parents gave statements about what had happened at the marina in Salou before being informed that their son had died. Psychologists were called in to offer support to the family.

The man is the 14th person to have died off the Tarragona coast so far this summer, and the first to die in September.
